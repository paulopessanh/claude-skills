# Anatomy of a brand ecosystem

What a complete package holds, why each part exists, and how much to trust each claim. Confidence: 🟢 documented practice, 🟡 common practice with weak evidence, 🔴 folklore.

## The parts

| Part | Question it answers | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Story and purpose | Why does this brand exist, in words a stranger repeats | 🟢 Aaker, Kapferer |
| Pétreos and vetados | What never changes and what never appears | 🟡 studio practice |

## Why one source

Two documents that repeat a number will diverge the first time one is edited. A package where tokens, assets and manual are
all derived from one file cannot disagree, and an auditor can recompute any number from that file. 🟢 This is the single
source of truth principle applied to identity.

## What the audit can and cannot prove

It proves internal consistency: colours used exist in the palette, no vetoed hue appears, text meets the declared contrast,
the manual states what the source states. It does not prove the brand is good, distinctive, legally free or printable.
Those need people, a trademark search and a printer's proof. 🟢
